1. Who we are

Fireroz Care, Dadanpur, Todabhim, Karauli, Rajasthan 321611, India. Contact: firerozofficial@gmail.com. Grievance: firerozcare@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores

Information you type or add yourself: family member names, medicines, doses and times, taken/missed marks, appointments, health record notes, photos and files. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Fireroz Care has no server and no account system, so the developer does not receive, access or hold this data.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you of medicine times.
  • Camera: only when you scan a QR code or take a medicine/record photo.

No location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID is used.

3A. Suggestions you choose to send

If you tap "सुझाव / फीचर रिक्वेस्ट भेजें" and press Send, the category and the text you typed (and the app version) are sent over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script owned by us and delivered to our email inbox. Purpose: understanding which features people need and fixing problems. We do not ask for or store your name, phone number or device ID, and we cannot reply. Please do not write health or personal details; if you do, they remain your voluntary submission. Google's servers may technically process your IP address while delivering the request. We keep suggestions only as long as needed and delete one on request (email the grievance address). Sending is optional; the app works fully without it.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not share your health records with anyone; the only data that leaves your phone is a suggestion you choose to send (see 3A). No analytics, ads or third-party SDKs that collect data are included. Android auto-backup is turned off. If the app asks you to rate it, that uses Google Play's own review dialog; we do not receive anything from it. Backup files are created only if you choose "बैकअप बनाएं", and you control where they go.

5. Your control

Edit or delete any entry in the app. Settings → clear all data erases everything the app holds; uninstalling the app also removes it. Because the data never leaves your phone, we cannot recover it if you lose it, so keep a backup.

6. Children

For users aged 18 and over only.

7. Medical disclaimer

Fireroz Care is a record and reminder tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe or give medical advice, is not a telemedicine or emergency service, and reminders can be delayed or missed due to phone settings. In an emergency call 112.

8. Changes

If we later add accounts, cloud storage or any data collection, we will update this policy and ask for your fresh consent before using such features.
